# Feature-keyed literature facts for enclosure (Shell – Enclosure) A1–A3 GWP per m² floor area

Compiled 2026-11-16 for CEE226. This file fills gaps left by `envelope_intensities_sources.md`, which holds component intensities per m² of component. Read it together with that file, which is not repeated here.

**Leakage screen.** None of the sources below is, or is derived from, the Benke et al. 2025 harmonized WBLCA dataset (figshare 28462145), the CLF WBLCA Benchmark Study v2, or Life Cycle Lab / UW analyses of that dataset. The UW items used (Amany 2019 thesis, LMN blog) predate or are independent of that dataset. One caution: F10 is a real UW building (Hans Rosling Center, Seattle, 2020) that could also be one of the 292 project buildings. If it is, F10 is effectively a labelled example and should be dropped from the prompt.

**Conventions.** "per m² floor" means per m² of the gross floor area the source uses. A source's own boundary is kept: A1–A3, A1–A5, or A–C (life cycle without D). "Derived" means my arithmetic on sourced inputs, with the inputs shown. 1 ft² = 0.0929 m². 1 kgCO2e/ft² = 10.76 kgCO2e/m². RSI 1 = R-5.68.

## (b) Notes per gap

**Gap 1: archetype assemblies.**
- **Solid:** PNNL's CBECS-based analyses (F1–F4) give wall descriptions, WWR and roof type for 15 US prototypes. Their limits are vintage (post-1990 stock, not 2015–2023 new construction) and the CBECS lumping of "brick, stone, stucco".
- **Missing from CBECS:** laboratories, public-safety/civic buildings and transport hubs have no prototype and no wall-type statistics. Only CBECS-1992 aspect ratios exist for labs (2.23), public order & safety (1.85) and public assembly (1.88).
- **Residential:**
  - High-rise: window wall dominates (Canadian sources, qualitative).
  - Type V multifamily: the Census multifamily tables have no exterior-wall-material table (only framing and stories). NAHB and Census siding shares are single-family, so they are not used. No sourced cladding mix for Type V multifamily was found.
- **Office CW threshold:** the unitized-vs-stick crossover (~10–20 storeys) comes only from industry blogs (unverified).
- **Not found:** hardened civic envelopes and transport-hub roof systems.

**Gap 2: enclosure per floor area or as a share.**
- **Usable:**
  - LETI shares: facade 13–17 % of cradle-to-gate.
  - BDP Quadrangle: 23 % of upfront for Toronto MURBs.
  - LMN: 5–15 %.
  - Two Canadian One Click office models: ≈ 138–140 kgCO2e/m² floor for envelope over the life cycle (A–C), plus roof 7–74.
  - Rosling Center: ≈ 104 A1–A3 (Athena).
- **Conflict:** the shares run 5–26 %. The low end (LMN) is US practice opinion. The high end comes from CW offices that include B4–B5.
- **Inaccessible:**
  - Make Architects (precast facade 55–85 kgCO2e/m² GIA by plan form): only a search snippet; the site returned 403. Unverified, not carded.
  - City of Vancouver / Morrison Hershfield 2023, ZGF mid-rise case study, ZEBx: 403. ZEBx snippets show inconsistent envelope shares (11–18 % vs 26–36 %).
  - CLF-BC case studies: 404.
- **Not opened:** Röck et al. 2020 gives no element split in the material I could reach. No NA hospital or warehouse element breakdown was found. I2SL Labs2Zero collects lab A1–A3 by substructure, superstructure and enclosure, but publishes no numbers on its public page.

**Gap 3: facade systems.**
- **New numbers:**
  - Arup unitized CW baseline 327 (A1–A5, EU).
  - CSA CW vs GFRC + punched windows: 138 vs 74 per m² wall.
  - Zinc cladding skin, Ruukki 0.7 mm: 23.7 kgCO2e/m² A1–A3 ([One Click page](https://materials.oneclicklca.com/en/material/metal-facade-cladding-from-titanium-zinc/62c7de7999d46b160a826117)). A metal skin alone is not a tail driver. The Ruukki copper card reports 5.39/m² (0.71 kg/kg), which looks implausibly low and was not used.
- **Not obtained:**
  - CWCT 2022 methodology (paywalled; only a Camden planning extract cites 170 → 152 kgCO2e/m² facade options, unverified).
  - Payette Kaleidoscope numbers (interactive tool).
  - Hartwell & Overend.
- Terracotta, EIFS, stucco and tilt-up remain as in the existing file (estimates).

**Gap 4: geometry.**
- No published table of wall/floor ratios by storey count was found. The LETI form-factor table only appears in secondary copies for houses.
- F15–F16 therefore build ratios from PNNL floor-to-floor heights, CBECS aspect ratios and DOE prototype sizes.
- **Rules of thumb that follow:** wall/GFA ≈ 0.2 for big 1–2 storey plates; ≈ 0.3 for large towers on big plates; ≈ 0.4–0.6 for 3–6 storey buildings with 800–2,000 m² plates and for slender towers. Roof/GFA = 1/storeys.

**Gap 5: tool conventions.**
- **Solid:** XPS ban dates; One Click XPS cards (legacy vs low-GWP, 10×); Tally's GaBi 2018 basis.
- **Partial:**
  - Tally vs Athena stage split (one thesis).
  - Cormick (2017, Ryerson/Queen's MRP): One Click whole-building carbon "under half" of Tally's for an identical bill of materials. Search snippet only (Scribd), unverified, not carded.
  - Priopta's 2023 five-tool database comparison is gated behind an email form.
- **Not found:**
  - Tally's own XPS dataset and blowing agent.
  - Tally vs One Click generic aluminum and glass factors.
  - An authoritative statement of whether Tally's enclosure categories include roof structure. Treat this as modeler-dependent (F20).
- **Cross-check:** the CSA mid-rise roof insulation (R-30 XPS) reads ≈ 26 kgCO2e/m² floor. With roof/GFA ≈ 0.167 that is ≈ 156/m² roof ≈ 30 per m² @ RSI-1, which matches HFC-era XPS (CLF2023 HFC XPS 22–37) and suggests the One Click default in 2022–23 Canadian work was not the low-GWP product.

**Gap 6: renovation scope.**
- **Found:** definitions (EPBD 25 % of envelope; LEED "significant envelope modifications") and two cases showing that when the envelope is in scope it is replaced in full and becomes the dominant element.
- **Not found:** statistics on the share of US major renovations that replace windows or roofs, or a code definition of "minor renovation". Tenant improvement / LEED ID+C scope is interior-only by definition, so enclosure ≈ 0 unless storefront glazing is included (an inference, not a sourced number).

**Gap 7: tails.**
- No published NA building was found with enclosure > 200 A1–A3 per m² floor. The CSA mid-rise office reaches ≈ 212 only on an A–C basis with replacements and an HFC-era XPS roof.
- Tail cards F23–F25 are derived from sourced components and should be labeled as such.
- Metal skins (zinc ~24/m²) do not create tails by themselves. Area-heavy aluminum (CW, fins), heavy precast or stone, and HFC foam on large roofs do.
